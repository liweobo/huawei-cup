# Assumptions Before Network Experiments

All assumptions below are model restrictions, not additional original facts. No answer sources are used.

- A01 PLANAR_COORDINATE_ASSUMPTION: XLS X/Y metres define a local Cartesian approximation. Euclidean lengths are neither road distances nor geodesics. Raster pixels/DPI are never used to measure length.
- A02 FULL_OFFDIAGONAL_SERVICE_POLICY: serve every positive off-diagonal OD underground, with secondary-station local access. This meets maximum park diversion and is deliberately stronger than the requested optional treatment of intra-area traffic. Cost comparisons are conditional on this fixed service policy, not a proof of an unrestricted global optimum.
- A03 INTRA_REGION_SURFACE_ONLY: preserve the two diagonal OD entries as local surface service. Their underground path is empty. Internal region travel is not inferable from a representative centre and is excluded from the computed transport-cost boundary; unknown intra-region distance is not called a measured zero.
- A04 SPLIT_REGION_ALLOCATION: a region may use multiple nearby secondary stations, with the same assignment fractions applied to sending and receiving flows. This is divisible freight allocation, not duplicated demand. Polygon-wide coverage is not claimed.
- A05 CANDIDATE_LOCATION_RESTRICTION: deterministic candidate secondary sites are original region centres; when a site's throughput requires an additional station, use successive 150 m east offsets. Every allocated centre is checked against the actual displaced site and 3 km radius. Primary interchange sites start 250 m north of their secondary. This restrictive constructive layout excludes most continuous station placements and shared multi-secondary hubs. It is computationally transparent, not an engineering siting recommendation. Offset sensitivities at 125/500 m are diagnostic.
- A06 HIERARCHY_RESTRICTION: one primary interchange per secondary. Primaries have zero local ground exchange; regional ground exchange occurs at secondaries, whose small-vehicle last-mile traffic is negligible under the original simplification. There are no secondary-secondary or secondary-to-other-primary links. Primary merging/demotion is evaluated only as a rejection diagnostic unless its ground/dispatch feasibility and changed flows can be proved.
- A07 GEOMETRIC_CANDIDATE_EDGE_ASSUMPTION: allowed physical tunnels are every park-primary pair, selected primary-primary pairs, and each own primary-secondary pair. Straight-line corridors are hypothetical, with no obstacle/crossing/turn-radius engineering verification. A distance cutoff is not a construction law. A sparse primary candidate search set is a heuristic restriction: geometric nearest-neighbour edges plus a connecting tree, independent of OD zeros. No candidate is treated as built before explicit selection.
- A08 DISPATCH_SCOPE: apply at most 90 departures/day in aggregate at each constructed primary/secondary station, including transit departures. Parks are external facilities, not the priced primary/secondary nodes; no park-wide dispatch limit is assumed. Each park tunnel still obeys directional line headway. The alternative park-wide 90-train interpretation is separately tested and would limit the amount of park freight accepted underground.
- A09 TRAIN_CONFIGURATION: 8 vehicles is the declared design maximum, giving 80 t on park-primary arcs and 40 t elsewhere. Four-vehicle sensitivity gives 40/20 t. A fractional flow relaxation is not a timetable. Static routing reserves one train per available outgoing arc: sum(flow/train_payload) <= 90 - outgoing_degree, sufficient for the rounded-up arc departures to be <=90, but not a daily-clearing proof.
- A10 COST_CONVERSION: annual depreciation 1% divided by 365 days; never also divide by the 100-year life. Physical bidirectional tunnels counted once. Access movement from region centre to secondary is priced at 1 yuan/(tonne km) as an explicit local straight-distance cost proxy; no road-cost claim. Internal diagonal movement cost is outside this evaluable boundary.
- A11 CAPACITY_DIRECTION: each direction has its own line headway capacity, with symmetric physical tunnel dimensions. All selected tunnels initially double-track. Four-track doubles directional line capacity but not station dispatch capacity; a sensitivity cannot cure a station bottleneck by silently changing node capacity.
- A12 Q3: failures remove one complete physical tunnel, both arcs. Directional surge multiplies only park 1's outgoing original OD by 1.10 or 1.25; no probabilities. Operational time is separately evaluated under an all-freight-available-at-start scenario and explicit legal queue actions, not inferred from aggregate capacity.
- A13 Q4: t=0 is the supplied day; year y uses 1.05^y. Eight years use equal lengths of construction work with a tunnel allowed to span years but unavailable until fully built. Stations commission with their first usable incident infrastructure; all operational links have commissioned endpoints. Incomplete service stays on a labeled surface fallback, not deleted demand. An intermediate year's failure to meet congestion/full-service targets is reported, even if surviving underground flows respect capacity.
- A14 EXPANSION: later copies/additional stations/corridors are scenarios distinct from the Q2 design. A capacity lower bound or modular scaling proposal is not a verified 30-year expansion timetable.

These restrictions permit real numerical evidence, but may produce PARTIAL task status. They cannot justify violating a hard constraint or calling a best-found design globally optimal.
