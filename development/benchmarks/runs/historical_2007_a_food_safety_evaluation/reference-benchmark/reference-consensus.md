## Consensus conclusion

The references strongly confirm that 2007A is an absolute probabilistic exposure-and-compliance problem. They do not justify treating it as a multi-criteria ranking exercise. They also provide demonstrated failure modes in which a mathematically computed output is attached to the wrong physical comparator or stronger claim. That evidence supports a narrowly scoped target/output-semantics guard, not a new evaluation algorithm or general risk-assessment framework.
