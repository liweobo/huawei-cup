## Evidence chain

- **Source/input:** AMOS, airport video and 100 highway screenshots. The video, matched labels, lane dimensions and scene points used only in the paper remain REFERENCE_ONLY_INPUT.
- **Target / estimation:** Q1 fits contemporaneous MOR to meteorology; Q2 estimates same-time MOR from image features; Q3 claims metric MOR from contrast, dark-channel transmission and road geometry.
- **Forecast target / origin:** Q4 uses all 100 Q3 values from 06:30:26–07:39:11. The last-frame boundary is inferable, but no formal issuance origin is stated: FORECAST_ORIGIN_UNSPECIFIED.
- **Availability / sequence:** Q4 uses only the estimated highway sequence. Future weather and observed future targets are not used.
- **Strategy:** B FULL_SEQUENCE_EXTRAPOLATION. ARIMA and GM(1,1) produce recursive/curve extrapolations; a cubic polynomial, despite being called linear regression, supplies the 150m crossing.
- **Validation / metric:** Q4 has IN_SAMPLE_FIT only. No earlier pseudo-origin backtest, persistence comparator, controlled future-error set or horizon-specific future metric is reported.
- **Uncertainty / claim:** No future prediction interval or coverage. The cubic fit has R²=0.0637 yet is extrapolated to a claimed 08:50:56 crossing (p49); crossing status UNVERIFIED.

## Q1

Pages 12–22 aggregate the two AMOS episodes to 15-minute records, screen variables by correlation, and fit separate spring/winter additive nonlinear regressions by a genetic algorithm. The same observations are used to select functional forms, optimize coefficients and display fit. Reported final R² values are 0.5182 and −0.0381 (p22), despite the accompanying reliability claim. Classification: TRAIN_FIT_ONLY, contemporaneous estimation rather than future forecasting.

## Q2

Pages 23–35 extract one key frame per minute and align it to one-minute visibility records, replace isolated abnormal frames with adjacent-minute images, and remove continuous bad-image runs. The method selects an 8×8 window size and the ten image regions most correlated with MOR, fits a three-feature linear model, then trains a BP network on its residuals.

No complete LOCALDATE/UTC CREATEDATE conversion, matching tolerance or drift policy is given: TEMPORAL_ALIGNMENT_UNVERIFIED. Appendix p60 explicitly says that, because the sample is small, the training data are used for testing and fresh data would normally be required. The same 434 samples also drive feature/window selection and fitting. Q2 is TRAIN_FIT_ONLY; R²=0.8342 is not independent estimation performance and is not Q4 forecast evidence.

## Q3 scale

Pages 36–43 detect road lines, infer a vanishing point, locate a farthest distinguishable row at a 0.05 contrast threshold, estimate transmission by dark-channel prior and calculate MOR≈3/k. Camera scaling uses a generic 6m lane divider and selected scene points A(203,234), B(229,268); C0=1 is explicitly assumed. Generic lane length is EXTERNAL_SOURCE, while its scene correspondence, the selected points and optical assumptions are AUTHOR_ASSUMPTION. All remain REFERENCE_ONLY_INPUT.

The paper reports values concentrated around 47m ±10m. An explicit conditional metric model exists, but the scene application and highway MOR accuracy have no independent validation. Output: ABSOLUTE_MOR_METRES; identifiability: PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED.

## Q4

Pages 44–49 fit ARIMA, GM(1,1) and a cubic polynomial to the complete derived series. ARIMA and GM predict short-run deterioration, while the cubic extrapolation predicts clearing; the disagreement is not resolved through held-out future observations. The crossing is obtained from the weak cubic fit rather than an externally validated forecast. Numerical comparison with the frozen run is NOT_DIRECTLY_COMPARABLE. Findings are G2/G3/G4/G5 and do not demonstrate G1.
