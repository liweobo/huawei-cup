## Q1

Pages 7–14: Pearson/Spearman screening followed by linear/nonlinear multiple regressions. One-minute means; missing quarter-minute records replaced by another same-minute observation. Each date is fitted separately, explicitly because seasons differ (p13); the second date is **not** an event holdout. R² 0.9168/0.9679 are fit statistics: TRAIN_FIT_ONLY. The paper calls this short-term prediction without establishing any future horizon or available future weather. Page 26 honestly reports negative/poor low-visibility estimates and seasonal instability. No explicit sensor-ceiling treatment was located.

## Q2

Pages 15–20: sample every 24 frames, then one representative per minute; aggregate four AMOS records into a minute, producing 463 paired records. Table p16 uses Beijing clock, but precise video drift, UTC CREATEDATE relation and tolerance are not established: TEMPORAL_ALIGNMENT_UNVERIFIED. The assumption that visibility is constant within a minute and manual removal of visibly mismatched samples appear on p6.

The target is explicitly 0.8 RVR_1A + 0.2 MOR_1A (pp15–16), split into nine classes with denser low-visibility bins. The 8:2 weighting is AUTHOR_ASSUMPTION, not an official MOR definition. Repeated-image augmentation to 300/class is described (p17); partition order and original-image grouping are unclear, so duplication across splits is a risk, not proven leakage. Split classification: UNKNOWN. Reported success rates vary: 78.8% p19, 77.75% in the p20 table, about 77.8% abstract/conclusion. Q2 predicts classes from observed later frames; this is still same-time estimation.

## Q3 scale and formula

Page 6 explicitly assumes 6m road stripes, 9m gaps and **6m camera height**. They are AUTHOR_ASSUMPTION / REFERENCE_ONLY_INPUT even where later prose says “known”. Pages 21–23 use Koschmieder/Duntley, C = C0 exp(−beta d), **C0=1 assumed**, threshold 0.05, MOR≈3/beta. The beta used is ln(1/t)/d, with t from dark-channel prior (15×15 window, weight 0.85) and d from perspective/cross-ratio geometry.

Page 23 infers d≈11m using the assumed camera height; projection/pixel quantities are converted through that scale. Merely computing a cross-ratio does not independently verify a physical height or depth. Claimed highway MOR is roughly 75–90m (p24). Classification: ABSOLUTE_MOR_METRES; PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED, conditional on assumptions, with no independent metre calibration or labelled highway error evaluation.

## Q4

All 100 inputs are legitimate possible history at an end-sequence origin; this alone is not leakage. Fit does not validate the extended acceleration or crossing time.
